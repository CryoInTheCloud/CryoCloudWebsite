# Welcome to StratusGeo: Empowering collaborative Earth science in the cloud!
StratusGeo (formerly CryoCloud) is a NASA-supported community and cloud platform built in partnership with the International Interactive Computing Collaboration ([2i2c](https://2i2c.org/)) and designed to advance collaborative, data-intensive Earth science across all disciplines. Since 2022, we have grown from a cryosphere-focused project into a community ecosystem that supports hundreds of domestic and international scientists across geoscience disciplines. Our goal is to empower Earth scientists by providing the community and technical mechanisms that accelerate time to science for their broad range of data workflows. **Whether you're looking to build workflows, share insights, or connect with others, you belong here.**

## 🚀 Our Mission  
StratusGeo embraces a broad vision—breaking down silos, fostering intellectual generosity, and empowering scientists to make the most of NASA's vast data resources through streamlined data workflows.

We aim to create an welcoming, interconnected research ecosystem that:
- **Reduces barriers:** Simplifies cloud adoption with cost-efficient and user-friendly tools and data workflows
- **Fosters collaboration:** Brings together data producers, engineers, computational experts, and domain scientists in a shared virtual space  
- **Drives innovation:** Accelerates impactful science with cutting-edge workflows and a commitment to open science  
- **Promotes sustainability:** Ensures long-term access to state-of-the-art computing and data resources
